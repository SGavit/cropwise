# Crop Library Source Notes

## Tamil Nadu Agricultural University Agritech Portal

The [Water Requirements of Agricultural Crops](https://agritech.tnau.ac.in/agriculture/agri_irrigationmgt_waterrequirements.html) reference lists indicative surface-irrigation totals of **500 mm for maize** over 100 days, **510 mm for groundnut** over 105 days, and **320 mm for soybean** over 85 days. CropWise will present these as **seasonal planning references**, not irrigation prescriptions, because actual applications depend on rainfall, soil texture, crop stage, and local extension advice.

The [TNAU irrigated groundnut guide](https://agritech.tnau.ac.in/agriculture/CropProduction/Oilseeds/oilseeds_groundnut01.html) identifies April–May, June–July, and July–August seasonal planting windows for its regional varieties. The application will use broad, localized **seasonal windows** and explicitly instruct growers to confirm variety and district timing with local agricultural extension support.

## Product-Content Boundaries

Crop-library water percentages are a relative dashboard indicator derived from the indicative seasonal planning totals. They are not a chemical, fertilizer, pest-control, or crop-treatment instruction. The library will retain visible guidance to verify local sowing dates, irrigation plans, and soil results with a Krishi Vigyan Kendra or qualified agricultural officer.

## Additional Profile References

The [ICAR Maize page](https://icar.org.in/en/crop-science/maize) describes maize’s food, feed, and baby-corn value paths and reports that maturity varies materially by product type and agro-climatic zone. The library will therefore use broad, farmer-readable timing and purpose guidance rather than presenting a single harvest date.

The [TNAU irrigated groundnut guide](https://agritech.tnau.ac.in/agriculture/CropProduction/Oilseeds/oilseeds_groundnut01.html) emphasizes fine tilth, soil-test-led nutrient planning, moisture at flowering, pegging, and pod development, and maturity checks before harvest. CropWise will summarize these as field-care checkpoints, not exact fertilizer or pesticide prescriptions.

The [Vikaspedia soybean cultivation resource](https://agriculture.vikaspedia.in/viewcontent/agriculture/crop-production/package-of-practices/oilseeds/a-comprehensive-guide-to-soybean-cultivation?lgn=en) is retained as a public agricultural reference for soybean good-practice material. CropWise will keep its soybean profile limited to broad establishment, moisture, harvest, and local-advice considerations until crop- and district-specific guidance is confirmed.
